Holding has a cost.

ALTAR is not a coin you hold and forget. If you hold it and do not offer for 7 days, the altar takes 1% of your coins per quiet day as tithe, into its share, which feeds the Feast. Offer once and the clock resets. The Oracle and the altar itself never pay tithe. Full detail on the Ritual page.
