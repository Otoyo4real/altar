Why no whale can take the altar

The altar answers a crowd, not a fortune.

The whale cap. No wallet can offer more than 0.05% of supply in one round. A whale and a minnow share the same ceiling.

Reputation. A new wallet can offer only the 10 minimum. Your cap grows with every round you hold, and reaches the full 0.05% after seven rounds. Splitting across fresh wallets does not help. Each new wallet starts at the minimum.

The crowd. The round needs at least forty distinct offerers. One wallet counts as one, however big it is.

Once per round. Each wallet can offer only once per round. There is no second offering.

The example. A whale holds 40% of all ALTAR. In one round it can offer at most 0.05% of supply, which is — ALTAR while the line is — ALTAR. The line to pass is 2% of supply, so it would take 40 wallets offering at the cap to reach it. One whale counts as one.
